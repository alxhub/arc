# ARC-TRK-ANT-01

ARC-TRK-ANT-01 is the flexible antenna for an ARC trackside NFC reader. It sits beneath the track and connects to the reader board through a narrow tail. The reader can use it to detect a tagged vehicle passing overhead.

The antenna is copper etched into the flex, with no electronic components to assemble. Its two connections mate with the reader's antenna port.
